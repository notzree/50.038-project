Target notes

Current target definition
- Unit of prediction: (track_id, region)
- Label: appears_in_region
  - 1 if the track appears at least once in that region's top200 chart
  - 0 otherwise

Why this target
- Directly supported by available chart data.
- Works for region-level prediction and map-style product output.
- Keeps the first production model simple and explainable.

Known limitations
- Coarse binary target; does not represent timing or longevity yet.
- Strong class imbalance expected for region-level pairs.

Next target upgrades
- Time-window labels (e.g., appears within 7/30 days).
- Longevity/regression labels (days on chart, peak rank).
